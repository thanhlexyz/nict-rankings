About

What is counted

The only source is OpenAlex. A person's papers are all works of their OpenAlex author ID(s), whatever affiliation is printed on them: being at NICT is not required, being the right person is.

  • Who is the right person: IDs confirmed in roster.csv (openalex_ids; an ORCID gives one directly). Where none is confirmed, the IDs are provisional: those OpenAlex attached to the person's name on works printing NICT. Such people are listed on the Open issues page with each ID's works and affiliations, until the IDs are confirmed.
  • Citations are OpenAlex's count for each paper, summed. h-index and i10-index are computed from those papers only.
  • Received since {{ $o.from_year }} sums OpenAlex's per-year counts of those papers (it keeps the last ten years). Papers since {{ $o.from_year }} are papers published from {{ $o.from_year }} on, with their own citations and h-index.
  • Own page (unofficial): papers listed on a person's own page (self_reported.csv) are added when OpenAlex does not have them yet, with no citation figure, and tagged.
  • A row in paper_overrides.csv can count or leave out one paper, with a note.

Not applied: self-citations and citations from co-authors or the same lab are not removed.

Journals and conferences

  • Journals: the SCImago Journal Rank table (best quartile: Q1 if the journal is Q1 in any of its categories), matched by ISSN where OpenAlex gives one, else by exact title. A journal not in the table is shown as "not in SCImago", not as a lower quartile.
  • Conferences: the CORE ranking at the time of the paper: the newest edition earlier than the paper's year (2021 → CORE2020), and when that edition does not list the conference, the nearest older edition, then later ones. Editions used: CORE2014, 2017, 2018, 2020, 2021, 2023 and ICORE2026. Matched by full name, or by acronym plus most of the name's words. A conference found in no edition is "unranked"; CORE's Australasian and national ranks count as unranked too. Example: IFIP/IEEE IM 2021 is A (CORE2018), while the 2023 list has it as B.
  • Preprints, patents, books, theses and venues that could not be classified are counted in their own columns. Hand fixes go in venue_overrides.csv.

Class order

When papers are ordered by class: Conf. A* > Journal Q1 > Conf. A > Journal Q2 > Conf. B > the rest (Q3, Q4, Conf. C, unranked, preprints, other).

Limits

  • OpenAlex indexes fewer papers than Google Scholar (grey literature, theses, some domestic and conference papers), so totals are lower than Scholar's. Google Scholar is not used.
  • OpenAlex merges and splits Japanese names often. Without a confirmed author ID a namesake can be merged in; the same paper can also appear twice (two versions), each with its own citations.
  • A person whose papers OpenAlex has attributed to another profile, or to none, is missing those papers.
