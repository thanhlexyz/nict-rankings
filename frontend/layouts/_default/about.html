About

What is counted

Each person's Google Scholar profile lists papers with a citation count. A profile can also hold papers that others wrote (added by hand, or merged into one's own entries). This site recounts the profile paper by paper and counts only those that name the person as an author.

  1. Verified: an independent record in OpenAlex (matched by exact title, year within one) lists the person as an author, by author ID confirmed in roster.csv or by name.
  2. Named in Scholar's list: no independent record was found, but the author list that Scholar prints for the paper names the person.
  3. Unconfirmed: the author list is cut ("...") and does not show the person, and there is no independent record. Shown, not counted.
  4. Author list does not name the person: the OpenAlex record, or Scholar's complete author list, has other names only. Shown, not counted.
  5. Outside the period at NICT: for a person who has left, only papers published within the years set in roster.csv (period_from, period_to) are counted. Shown, not counted.
  6. Confirmed by the maintainer: a row in paper_overrides.csv overrides the rule, with a note.

Counted citations are those of verified, named and maintainer-confirmed papers. Share is counted divided by the total on the profile. The h-index and i10-index are recomputed from the counted papers only. This is a statement about author lists, not about intent: a paper can be missing from a person's author list for ordinary reasons (a different spelling of the name, a database that has not caught up), so every excluded paper is listed with its reason on the person's page.

Not applied: self-citations and citations from co-authors are not removed. Only whether the paper is the person's own is checked.

Journals and conferences

  • Journals: the SCImago Journal Rank table (best quartile: Q1 if the journal is Q1 in any of its categories), matched by ISSN where OpenAlex gives one, else by exact title. A journal not in the table is shown as "not in SCImago", not as a lower quartile.
  • Conferences: the CORE conference ranking (ICORE2026, then CORE2023 and CORE2021 for conferences no longer listed), matched by full name, or by acronym plus most of the name's words. A conference that is not found is "unranked"; CORE's Australasian and national ranks are counted as unranked here. The latest list is applied to all years.
  • Preprints, patents, books, theses and venues that could not be classified are counted in their own columns. Hand fixes go in venue_overrides.csv.

Last five years ({{ $o.from_year }} on)

Scholar's "since {{ $o.from_year }}" figure counts citations received since {{ $o.from_year }} by all of a person's papers. The counted figures for this window are for papers published since {{ $o.from_year }}, because Scholar does not give a per-paper history. Both are shown and are not the same quantity. Per-year bars on the Analysis and person pages come from the profile's own "cited by" chart.

Monthly figures

The first points are from the screening workbook ("Total citation", 2026/06 and 2026/07). Every later snapshot of this site adds one point per person.

Sources and limits

  • Google Scholar profile pages (a manual snapshot; Scholar blocks automated search, so profile IDs are listed by hand).
  • Researchmap for people without a Scholar profile (papers and author lists), with citations from OpenAlex; such people have no claimed figure.
  • OpenAlex indexes fewer papers than Scholar (grey literature, theses, some conference papers): a missing record is not evidence either way, which is why papers whose Scholar author list names the person still count.
  • OpenAlex merges and splits Japanese names often; its author IDs are confirmed by hand.
